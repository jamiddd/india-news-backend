About Open Indian News

The same story, told fifteen ways. We show you all of them, once.

Open Indian News is an Android news app that groups every outlet's coverage of a story into a single item, summarises it, and shows how different publishers are framing the same facts. It is built and operated by a single independent developer in Assam, India.

How it works

Clustering, not scrolling.

Most Indian news apps show you the same event over and over, wrapped in advertising. We collapse it into one item, then show you what changed between the tellings.

  1. 01

    Cluster

    Every article published on the same event is matched and folded into a single story, whether two outlets covered it or forty.

    ↓
    One story
  2. 02

    Summarise

    The clustered coverage is condensed into a few bullets, enough to know what happened without opening anything.

    ↓
    •••Three bullets
  3. 03

    Compare the framing

    Each outlet's angle on that same story is set out side by side, so the difference between the tellings is the thing you can see.

What the app does

Story clustering

Articles published about the same event by different outlets are matched and folded into one story, so the same event is read once rather than scrolled past repeatedly.

Media framing

For stories covered by several outlets, the app sets out the angle each publisher led with, side by side.

Short summaries

Each story carries a short summary, so a reader can decide whether the full coverage is worth their time.

No advertising

The reading experience carries no ads: no banners, no interstitials, and nothing that follows you between stories.

Saved stories and digests

Save what you want to come back to, and have a digest delivered at the times of day you choose.

Daily games and services

Crossword, sudoku, word search, spelling bee, word ladder, Daily Word and a daily quiz, plus horoscope, word of the day, quote of the day, on this day and a community poll.

Where the news comes from

The app aggregates publicly available news feeds from Indian publishers. Every article links back to the original publisher, who remains the source and the owner of that reporting. Open Indian News does not write news of its own.

If you publish news and would like to be included, or removed, please get in touch. Requests are handled by a person, not a queue.

Found something broken, or think a story is being framed badly? Tell us. It goes straight to the person who builds the app, and you do not need an account.
