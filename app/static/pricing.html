Pricing

Open Indian News · Last updated: 2 October 2026

Open Indian News is free to read, with no ads. The news feed, story clustering, summaries, media framing, saved stories, notifications and today's daily games cost nothing. An optional Premium plan adds the extras listed below.

Premium plans

PlanPriceHow it is billed
Premium Monthly₹49 per monthRecurring. Renews automatically every month until you cancel.
Premium All-Time₹490One-time purchase. No renewal and no further charge.

Premium is bought inside the app through Google Play, and Google Play handles the payment, billing, renewal and cancellation. You can cancel Monthly at any time in the Google Play Store; it stays active until the end of the period you have paid for. Premium is tied to your Google account, and Restore purchases in the app brings it back on a new device. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy and Terms of Service. The price shown at checkout in Google Play is the price you pay; prices may change in future, which does not affect a plan you have already bought.

Donations

If you would rather keep using the free app than buy Premium, but would like to show your appreciation for an ad-free feed, you can make an optional donation towards running costs — servers, data and the services the app depends on.

A donation unlocks nothing. It grants no Premium features and no change of any kind inside the app. It is a contribution towards keeping the app running, not a purchase and not a Premium plan. Nobody is asked to donate in order to use any part of the free app.

Donations are processed by Razorpay and can be made on this site or from inside the app. Amounts are chosen by the donor. Donations are separate from Premium and are not made through Google Play. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy for how to request a refund, and our Terms of Service for the terms that apply.

No advertising in the feed

The reading experience carries no advertising and no interstitials, on every plan, free or Premium.
