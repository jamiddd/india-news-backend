Donations

The only payment the app accepts is an optional donation towards running costs — servers, data and the services the app depends on.

A donation unlocks nothing. It grants no features, no premium status, no removal of anything, and no change of any kind inside the app. It is a contribution towards keeping the app running, not a purchase. Nobody is asked to donate in order to use any part of the app.

Donations are processed by Razorpay and can be made on this site or from inside the app. Amounts are chosen by the donor. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy for how to request a refund, and our Terms of Service for the terms that apply.

No advertising in the feed

The reading experience carries no advertising, no interstitials and nothing that follows a reader between stories.
