What the app is

Open Indian News aggregates headlines and short snippets from Indian news publishers' public RSS feeds, groups coverage of the same story across outlets, and links out to the original articles. It is not a source of original journalism — it's a discovery/aggregation layer on top of third-party reporting.

Attribution and original sources

Every story shown links back to its original publisher. We display headlines, short snippets, and publish timestamps as provided in each publisher's own RSS feed — never full article text. Reading the complete article always happens on the original publisher's website, under that publisher's own terms.
