What the app is

Open Indian News aggregates headlines, short snippets and images from Indian news publishers' public feeds and web pages, groups coverage of the same story across outlets, and links out to the original articles. It is not a source of original journalism — it's a discovery/aggregation layer on top of third-party reporting.

Attribution and original sources

Every story shown links back to its original publisher. The app displays headlines, short snippets, images and publish timestamps taken from each publisher's public feed or article page — it does not display full article text. Reading the complete article always happens on the original publisher's website, under that publisher's own terms.

To build summaries, story groupings and framing comparisons, our servers fetch publicly accessible article pages and keep the text extracted from them for internal processing, including by an AI provider. We do not access paywalled or login-restricted content, and we do not republish that text. If you are a publisher and want your content excluded or removed, please contact us.

No warranty on content accuracy

Story summaries, categorization, and cross-source comparisons are generated automatically (in part using AI) from third-party headlines, snippets and article text. Story summaries and framing analysis are produced automatically, without a human reviewing each one. We make a good-faith effort at accuracy, but we don't independently verify third-party reporting, and we can't guarantee the app is free of errors, omissions, or delays relative to the original source. Always treat the linked original article as the authoritative version of a story.

AI-generated content and audio

Some content is produced with AI: story summaries and framing analysis (automatically, with no per-story human review), and the Daily Brief, Late-Night Wrap-up, timelines and explainers in the Context tab (which are reviewed by a person before publication). The Context tab's spoken audio is a synthetic AI voice, not a human reading. AI can make mistakes; treat all of it as a summary and check the linked original reporting for anything that matters.
