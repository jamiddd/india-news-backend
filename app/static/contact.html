What to write about

  • Something is broken — a story that won't load, a game that won't open, a crash. Telling us your device model and roughly when it happened helps a lot.
  • A story is wrong or misleading — you can also report a story from inside the app, which sends us the story directly.
  • Your data — a copy of what we hold, or deletion of your account. You can delete your account yourself in Settings; email us if that doesn't work. See our Privacy Policy.
  • Donations and refunds — see the Refund & Cancellation Policy. Include the payment ID (starting pay_) if you have it.
  • You publish news and want to be included, or want to be removed — we aggregate publicly available feeds and will honour a removal request from a publisher.
