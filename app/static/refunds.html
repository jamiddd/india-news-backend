Refund & Cancellation Policy

Open Indian News · Last updated: 26 November 2026

Open Indian News is free to read, with no ads. No payment is needed to read stories, save them, receive notifications, or play today's daily games. The app accepts two kinds of payment, and they are handled differently:

  • Premium (Premium Monthly, ₹49 per month, or Premium All-Time, ₹490 one-time) is bought through Google Play. Refunds and cancellation are covered in the Premium sections below.
  • Donations are voluntary, processed by Razorpay, and unlock nothing. They are covered in the Donations sections below.

Premium: cancelling

Premium Monthly renews automatically every month until you cancel. You can cancel at any time in the Google Play Store (Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Open Indian News). Cancelling stops future renewals; Premium stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for. Deleting your Open Indian News account does not cancel a subscription — cancel it in Google Play first.

Premium All-Time is a one-time purchase. It does not renew and there is nothing to cancel.

Premium: free trial

The free 7-day Premium trial asks for no payment and is not a subscription, so there is nothing to cancel or refund. It ends by itself after 7 days; Premium then switches off unless you buy it.

Premium: refunds

Premium purchases are made, billed and refunded through Google Play, so Google Play's refund policy applies. To request a refund, use Google Play (Order history, or Google Play Help → Request a refund). If something has gone wrong, such as being charged twice or Premium not appearing after payment, please also contact us with your Google Play order number and we will help sort it out. We cannot see your card or UPI details; Google Play does not share them with us.

Donations are separate from Premium. A donation unlocks nothing. It grants no Premium features, no badge, and no change of any kind inside the app. It is a contribution towards running costs, nothing more.

Donations are non-refundable

Because a donation buys nothing, there is no product or service to return, and donations are generally non-refundable.

When we will refund anyway

We will refund a donation, in full, in any of these cases:

  • You were charged more than once for what you intended as a single donation.
  • You were charged an amount you did not intend — for example a mistyped custom amount.
  • You did not authorise the payment, or someone else used your payment method.
  • You simply changed your mind, and tell us within 7 days of the payment.

We are not going to argue about a donation. If you ask for one back within 7 days, you get it back.

How to request a refund

Email jamiddeka1@gmail.com with the payment ID from your payment confirmation (it starts with pay_), the amount, and the approximate date. The payment ID is the fastest way for us to find the transaction; without it we may need to ask you for more details.

How long it takes

  • We aim to respond to a refund request within 3 business days.
  • Once approved, the refund is issued to the original payment method — we cannot refund to a different account, card or UPI ID.
  • Your bank or UPI provider typically takes a further 5–7 business days to credit it. That part is outside our control.

Cancelling a donation

A donation is a one-time payment. It is not a subscription, has no recurring charge, and making one does not set up any future charge, so there is nothing to cancel. (Premium Monthly is a recurring subscription; see “Premium: cancelling” above.)
