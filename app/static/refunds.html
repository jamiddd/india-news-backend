Refund & Cancellation Policy

Open Indian News · Last updated: 4 September 2026

Open Indian News is free to use. There is no subscription, no paid tier, and no purchase of any kind required to read stories, save them, receive notifications, or play the daily games.

The only payment the app accepts is a voluntary donation. A donation unlocks nothing. It grants no features, no premium status, no badge, and no change of any kind inside the app. It is a contribution towards running costs, nothing more.

Donations are non-refundable

Because a donation buys nothing, there is no product or service to return, and donations are generally non-refundable.

When we will refund anyway

We will refund a donation, in full, in any of these cases:

  • You were charged more than once for what you intended as a single donation.
  • You were charged an amount you did not intend — for example a mistyped custom amount.
  • You did not authorise the payment, or someone else used your payment method.
  • You simply changed your mind, and tell us within 7 days of the payment.

We are not going to argue about a donation. If you ask for one back within 7 days, you get it back.

How to request a refund

Email jamiddeka1@gmail.com with the payment ID from your payment confirmation (it starts with pay_), the amount, and the approximate date. The payment ID is the fastest way for us to find the transaction; without it we may need to ask you for more details.

How long it takes

  • We aim to respond to a refund request within 3 business days.
  • Once approved, the refund is issued to the original payment method — we cannot refund to a different account, card or UPI ID.
  • Your bank or UPI provider typically takes a further 5–7 business days to credit it. That part is outside our control.

Cancellations

There is nothing to cancel. Open Indian News has no subscriptions, no recurring payments, and no auto-renewals. A donation is a one-time payment, and making one does not set up any future charge.

If we ever introduce a paid subscription, this policy will be updated before it launches, and the cancellation terms will be stated here.
