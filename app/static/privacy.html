Privacy Policy

Open Indian News · Last updated: 4 September 2026

This policy explains what information Open Indian News ("the app", "we", "us") collects, why, and how you can have it deleted. We've written it to describe exactly what the app actually does — not a generic template.

Information we collect

When you sign in (with Google, or with an email and password) via Firebase Authentication, we store:

  • Your email address and display name, as provided by your sign-in method.
  • A stable account identifier (Firebase's user ID) and which sign-in method you used.
  • Your app preferences — theme, accent color, language filter, and which news categories you've enabled or added — so they carry over if you sign in on another device.

We also store, once you are signed in:

  • Stories you save, so they follow you to another device (see below).
  • Which stories you have opened, and roughly how long you spent on them, to rank the "For You" feed. This never leaves our server and is not sold or shared.

We do not collect your location, contacts, device identifiers, or your browsing history outside the app.

Donations

The app is free. It accepts an optional donation, which unlocks nothing — see our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

We never see or store your card, UPI ID, or any other payment details. Payment is handled entirely by Razorpay on their own page; we only receive confirmation from them that a payment happened, its amount, and its transaction ID. If you were signed in when you donated, we store that transaction alongside your account ID so we can find it if you ask for a refund. Donating while signed out is possible, and then we hold no link to you at all.

Saved stories

Stories you save are kept on your device, and — if you are signed in — also synced to our server so they appear on any device you sign in to. If you are not signed in, they stay on your device only, and uninstalling the app or clearing its data loses them permanently.

Deleting your account (Settings → Delete Account) removes the server-side copy along with everything else.

What we don't do

The app has no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, and no advertising SDK of any kind. We don't track your behavior in the app, and we don't share or sell any information to advertisers or data brokers, because there aren't any integrated.

Third parties

We use Firebase Authentication, a service provided by Google, to handle sign-in. Google processes your email, display name, and authentication credentials as part of providing that service, under Google's own privacy terms. We don't share your data with any other third party.

News content

The app displays headlines, short snippets, and links to original articles from third-party news publishers. We don't store or serve full article text — we always link back to the original publisher's site, and every story is attributed to its source. Reading full articles happens on the publisher's own site, subject to their own privacy policy.

How long we keep your data

We keep your account information for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account (Settings → Delete Account, in the app), your account row and preferences are permanently removed from our database, and your Firebase Authentication identity is deleted — immediately, not after a delay.

Your rights

  • Access — you can see your current preferences at any time in the app's Settings screen.
  • Correction — update your display name/preferences directly in the app.
  • Deletion — delete your account and all associated server-side data at any time via Settings → Delete Account. This is immediate and permanent.

If you're in India, these rights are consistent with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. If you have any question about your data that isn't covered by the in-app controls above, contact us at the address below.

Children's privacy

Open Indian News is a general-audience news app and is not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 18. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
