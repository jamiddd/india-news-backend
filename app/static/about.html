About Us

Open Indian News · Last updated: 5 September 2026

Open Indian News is an Android news app that groups every outlet's coverage of a story into a single item, summarises it, and shows how different publishers are framing the same facts. It is built and operated by a single independent developer in Assam, India.

What the app does

  • Story clustering. Articles published about the same event by different outlets are matched and folded into one story, so the same event is read once rather than scrolled past repeatedly.
  • Media framing. For stories covered by several outlets, the app sets out the angle each publisher led with, side by side.
  • Summaries. Each story carries a short summary, so a reader can decide whether the full coverage is worth their time.
  • No advertising in the feed. The reading experience carries no ads.
  • Saved stories and scheduled digests, at times the reader chooses.
  • Daily games and services — crossword, wordle, sudoku, word search, spelling bee, word ladder, a daily quiz, plus horoscope, word of the day, quote of the day, on this day and a community poll.

Where the news comes from

The app aggregates publicly available news feeds from Indian publishers. Every article links back to the original publisher, who remains the source and the owner of that reporting. Open Indian News does not write news of its own.

If you publish news and would like to be included, or removed, please get in touch — requests are handled by a person, not a queue.

How it is funded

The app is free. There is no subscription, no premium tier and no paid feature. The only payment it accepts is an optional donation towards running costs, which unlocks nothing. See Pricing, Donate and our Refund & Cancellation Policy.
