Open Indian News

Indian news from many outlets, clustered into one story — and compared.

Most Indian news apps show you the same story fifteen times, wrapped in advertising. Open Indian News groups every outlet's coverage of a story into a single item, summarises it, and shows you how different publishers are framing the same facts.

What it does

Multi-outlet story clustering Coverage of the same event from different publishers is grouped into one story, so you read the event once instead of scrolling past it repeatedly.
Media framing analysis For stories covered by several outlets, we show the angle each publisher led with — the same facts, framed differently, side by side.
Summaries Each story carries a short summary, so you can decide whether to read the full coverage.
No ads in the feed The reading experience carries no advertising.
Saved stories and notification scheduling Save what you want to come back to, and choose the times of day you want a digest — as many or as few as you like.
Daily games A crossword, wordle, sudoku, word search, spelling bee, word ladder and a daily quiz — a fresh set every day.

Pricing

About

Open Indian News is built and operated by an independent developer in Assam, India. It aggregates publicly available news feeds from Indian publishers; all articles link back to the original publisher. If you publish news and would like to be included, or removed, please get in touch.
