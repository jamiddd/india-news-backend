How it works

Clustering, not scrolling.

Most Indian news apps show you the same event over and over, wrapped in advertising. We collapse it into one item — then show you what changed between the tellings.

  1. STEP 01

    Cluster

    Every article published on the same event is matched and folded into a single story, whether two outlets covered it or forty.

    ↓
    One story
  2. STEP 02

    Summarise

    The clustered coverage is condensed into a few bullets — enough to know what happened without opening anything.

    ↓
    Three bullets
  3. STEP 03

    Compare the framing

    Each outlet's angle on that same story is set out side by side, so the difference between the tellings is the thing you can see.

    Same facts, different lead

Features

Everything, at no cost.

Story clustering

Coverage of the same event from different publishers is grouped into one story, so you read the event once instead of scrolling past it repeatedly.

Framing analysis

For stories covered by several outlets, we show the angle each publisher led with — the same facts, framed differently, side by side.

Short summaries

Each story carries a short summary, so you can decide in seconds whether the full coverage is worth your time.

No ads in the feed

The reading experience carries no advertising, no interstitials, and nothing that follows you between stories.

Digests on your schedule

Save what you want to come back to, and pick the times of day you want a digest — as many or as few as you like.

Daily games

A fresh set every morning: crossword, wordle, sudoku, word search, spelling bee, word ladder and a daily quiz.
