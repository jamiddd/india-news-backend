Credits

Open Indian News · Last updated: 7 September 2026

Open Indian News aggregates other people’s work. None of the reporting is ours, and much of what the app and this site are made of — the dictionary behind the puzzles, the typefaces, the libraries — was given away freely by the people who made it. This page names them. The same credits appear in the app under Settings › Credits.

Content & data

  • Indian news publishers. Every story in the feed is reported, written and owned by the outlet it links to. We cluster and summarise; we do not report. Headlines and extracts are shown under fair dealing, each linking back to the publisher.
  • Wikipedia — Wikimedia Foundation and its contributors. On This Day historical events, under CC BY-SA 4.0.
  • APIVerve — daily quotations and crossword grids.
  • AstroJSON — daily horoscopes.
  • Claude, by Anthropic — story summaries, framing analysis, the daily poll and the daily quiz, all reviewed by a human before publication.

Words & puzzles

Daily Word, Spelling Bee, Word Ladder and Word Search are computed from a dictionary rather than generated. That dictionary is SCOWL — Spell Checker Oriented Word Lists, by Kevin Atkinson. Copyright 2000–2018 Kevin Atkinson, used and redistributed under SCOWL’s attribution-only terms. SCOWL itself draws on the 12Dicts package and the ENABLE word list (Alan Beale), Moby Words II (public domain), and Brian Kelk’s UK English Wordlist with Frequency Classification.

Open source software

The app and its backend stand on a great deal of freely given work.

  • Jetpack Compose, AndroidX and Material 3 — The Android Open Source Project (Apache 2.0). The entire interface, navigation, storage (Room), background work and the home screen widget (Glance).
  • Kotlin, Coroutines and kotlinx.serialization — JetBrains (Apache 2.0).
  • Ktor and OkHttp — JetBrains and Square (Apache 2.0).
  • Coil — Coil Contributors (Apache 2.0). Image loading and caching.
  • Lottie for Android — Airbnb (Apache 2.0). Plays the animated artwork.
  • Media3 / ExoPlayer — The Android Open Source Project (Apache 2.0).
  • Android YouTube Player — Pierfrancesco Soffritti (MIT).
  • FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, httpx, PostgreSQL and Redis — MIT, BSD and PostgreSQL licences. The backend that reads the feeds, clusters the stories and serves this site.
