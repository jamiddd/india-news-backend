Delete your account and data

Open Indian News · Last updated: 5 September 2026

You can delete your Open Indian News account at any time, and doing so permanently erases the data we hold about you. You do not need to contact us or wait for a support ticket — the deletion runs immediately, from inside the app.

In the app: Settings → scroll to Danger Zone → Delete account, and confirm. You will be asked to sign in again first, because deletion requires a fresh proof of identity — that is what stops anyone else deleting your account.

If you can no longer sign in

If you have lost access to the device or the sign-in method, email jamiddeka1@gmail.com from the address on the account and ask for deletion. We will verify that you control that address and complete it manually, normally within 7 days and at most within 30.

What is deleted

Deleting your account removes, permanently and immediately:

  • Your account record — email address, display name and account identifier — from both our database and Firebase Authentication.
  • Your saved stories.
  • Your app preferences, including theme, language filter and your category choices.
  • The sources you have chosen to follow or block.
  • Your reading history — which stories you opened, how long you spent, how far you scrolled — and the topic affinities derived from it.
  • Your push notification tokens, so no further notification can reach any device.
  • Your game sessions and your notification history.

This is not a flag marking the account inactive. The rows are deleted, and every table above is removed along with the account by the database itself, so nothing can be missed or left behind.

What is kept, and why

If you have ever donated, the donation record itself remains — the amount, the date and the payment provider’s transaction ID. Indian tax and accounting rules require us to keep a record of money actually received, and we cannot delete it on request.

Your link to it is severed. The account identifier stored against the donation is cleared when the account is deleted, so what remains is an anonymous record that a payment of some amount happened on some date. It cannot be traced back to you through anything we hold. We never had your card or UPI details in the first place — see the Privacy Policy.

Aggregate counts that contain no personal data — how many people opened a story, for instance — may persist, as they are not linked to any account.

Reading without an account

Signing in is optional. You can read the feed, search, and play the daily games without an account, and in that case there is nothing on our servers to delete: saved stories stay on your device, and uninstalling the app removes them.
